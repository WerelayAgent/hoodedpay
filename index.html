The financial layer for AI agents is here. HoodedPay gives it privacy. A non-custodial neobank where humans and agents transact confidentially, and every settlement is verified on pump.fun.

FIG 0.2

Private by default

Every transfer is confidential from the moment it leaves your account. Amounts are encrypted using ZK proofs, verifiable on-chain and invisible to observers.

FIG 0.3

Built for AI agents

Agent accounts resolve x402 payment requests autonomously. Programmable spending policies, MPP routing, and DID-based identity are all enforced on-chain.

FIG 0.4

Settled on pump.fun

Transactions land in 100ms blocks at a fraction of a cent, secured by Ethereum. Every settlement is permanently recorded: transparent infrastructure, private activity.

One account.
Two kinds of principals.

Human accounts and AI agent accounts live under the same protocol. Non-custodial, ZK-shielded, and governed by on-chain spending policies instead of server-side rules.
